About

Welcome to a place dedicated to three unforgettable anime series: Attack on Titan, My Hero Academia, and Fullmetal Alchemist. Each series offers its own unique world, memorable characters, intense battles, and powerful stories that have captivated fans around the world.

From the fight for humanity’s survival in Attack on Titan, to the journey of aspiring heroes in My Hero Academia, and the alchemical adventures of the Elric brothers in Fullmetal Alchemist, these series explore themes of friendship, sacrifice, determination, and the consequences of our choices.

Whether you’re a longtime fan or discovering these anime for the first time, this page is a place to appreciate their stories, characters, worlds, and the impact they’ve had on anime culture.
